# Henge fork scan, 2026-11-13

Scope: every fork of Leviidev/Henge, compared with upstream main e98920c through `gh api` compare reads (no clones, no pushes, no comments). Haiku subagents reviewed the per-fork diffs; the cloud thread then checked each shortlisted change against oliverames/Henge main 0070eae and our open upstream PRs #128-#157. Nothing here is implemented, built or run on the Duo.

## Totals

- 179 forks (175 direct, 4 forks of forks); 8 are new since the 2026-11-12 survey (`research-73-forks-madeira/FORKS.md`).
- 162 forks had no push since that survey and count as covered.
- 87 branches compared; 71 branches in 14 forks are ahead of upstream. Of those, 44 had nothing after the cutoff, 7 are upstream PR branches, 15 were not ahead, and 1 (Tomba247 `source`) shares no history with upstream.
- 20 branches in 10 forks were candidates: 84 new commits, 63 after dropping merges, copies of our PR #134 and a zh-CN sync. Haiku review produced 67 entries, 20 marked as candidates.
- All forks involved are GPL-2.0 per the GitHub license API, the same as upstream, so cherry-picks with authorship kept are allowed. No AGPL or unlicensed code was found.

Raw data on the Mac: `~/Developer/Forks/henge-worktrees/research-fork-scan-2026-11-13/` (`summary.md`, `shortlist.md`, `reviews-all.json`, `diffs/`, and full patches of the top picks in `evidence-2026-11-13/`).

## Ranked findings (checked against our main)

### 1. Release the high address holds before sizing a Mac's RAM (speed, memory)
Tomba247 [18ea2e05](https://github.com/Tomba247/Henge/commit/18ea2e05f67ea96b1e1bfef8d32fa9c38c15534f). Our `henge_ios_jit_prewarm` calls `henge_ios_hold_high()`, which reserves 40 + 16 + 4 GB of address space at 0x70-0x80 for Mac apps' allocators. Our code releases those holds only when QEMU starts (`henge-ios-jit.c:647`). `GuestMemory.prepare` (`Machines.swift:42`) then caps guest RAM at the largest free mapping minus 512 MB, which with the holds in place is the roughly 2.5 GB gap after the JIT view. Tomba247's change adds `henge_ios_release_high_room()` and calls it before measuring, skipping the release once a Mac app has been handed the holds.
- Status in ours: not present. Same gap confirmed in our source.
- Before taking it: read a Duo session log for `guest RAM ... largest mapping` to confirm the cap actually binds on the Duo. If it does, this is the biggest speed lever in the scan (more guest RAM, less guest paging).
- Drop the rest of that commit (it also withdraws a 2 GB code-cache option our tree never had).

### 2. Edge swipes go to the Mac first (Duo display, input)
pgabove [9c7fd1a5](https://github.com/pgabove/Henge/commit/9c7fd1a5d88e4915f5aaa7f6311172706535063a), one line: `.defersSystemGestures(on: .all)` on `SessionView` next to `.persistentSystemOverlays(.hidden)`. A first swipe from an edge reaches macOS (Dock, menu bar); a second swipe still opens iOS's.
- Status in ours: not present (no `defersSystemGestures` anywhere; `SessionView.swift:198-199` has the two neighbouring modifiers).
- Take only this line. The rest of the commit (USB touchscreen) is item 8.

### 3. HV_SYNC_WAIT on by default on iPhone (speed)
Tomba247 [104522b2](https://github.com/Tomba247/Henge/commit/104522b24b1955e6b89408347e21707503e11c5c). Turns on the existing `HV_SYNC_WAIT` knob (a CPU waiting on another's TLB acknowledgement sleeps after a short spin instead of spinning) for every session, with a Settings switch to turn it off.
- Status in ours: the knob exists (`core/machine.c:200`) but `EngineTuning.defaults` does not set it.
- Needs a Duo A/B before default-on. No measurement in the fork. Drop its mic-log hunk.

### 4. Spin briefly before sleeping on the translation lock (speed, JIT)
mohamedt1996 [e886dd6f](https://github.com/mohamedt1996/Henge/commit/e886dd6f20b76f50b74a9b1c2cead0c55387482b). Most of this commit is an independent version of the per-page exit list that upstream already has as 7131022, so our main has it (`hv_invalidate_code` walks `r->xhead[pg]`). What's new is a spin of up to 256 `yield`s with a `trylock` every 8th before `pthread_mutex_lock(&m->tr_mu)` in `lookup()`. The author reports 2,000-4,000 waits a second of 6-12 µs each on a tablet.
- Status in ours: the per-page list is redundant. The spin is not present (`core/dispatch.c:306` goes straight from trylock to lock).
- Our stats already count `tr_waits` and `tr_wait_ticks`, so a Duo A/B can measure it directly. Its "refuse a chain when the list is full" guard does not apply to our per-slot linked list.

### 5. Free the old pointer image after the swap (stability)
lunarduststudiodev-rgb [0291343](https://github.com/lunarduststudiodev-rgb/Henge/commit/0291343263b2c6d91da439e036c72fd0ecf22088). Our `cursorGlyph` (`devices/pvg_metal.m:2548`) frees `cursor` before taking `screen_lock` to install the new one, while `drawCursor` reads `cursor` without a lock. The fork frees the old buffer after the swap.
- Status in ours: the window exists. It only matters on the software-frame path (no display layer) when a new pointer image arrives during a draw.
- The fork's fix narrows the window but does not close it, because `drawCursor` still reads without `screen_lock`. A complete fix also reads the cursor fields under the lock. Small; reimplement rather than port, since this fork's branch is 202 commits behind upstream.

### 6. Mac-only 24-bit depth/stencil formats (GPU correctness)
lunarduststudiodev-rgb [c933ffe1](https://github.com/lunarduststudiodev-rgb/Henge/commit/c933ffe14c64a5dda29097a13cad731c7960e8b5) + [e855504e](https://github.com/lunarduststudiodev-rgb/Henge/commit/e855504e37011f353e1126911893dd5ef9119128). Maps `Depth24Unorm_Stencil8` (255) to `Depth32Float_Stencil8` and `X24_Stencil8` (262) to `X32_Stencil8`, which iOS Metal lacks. The author says this fixes magenta output in Chromium/ANGLE apps. That claim is unverified.
- Status in ours: no mapping for 255/262. The fork's code sits on `pvg_command.h` and `pvg_validate.h`, which our tree doesn't have, so this would be a port, not a cherry-pick.
- Before porting: a GPU trace (`Documents/trace-on`) of a Chromium app on the Duo showing formats 255/262 being refused.

### 7. Smaller items worth an A/B (speed)
From lunarduststudiodev-rgb e855504e. All would need hand-porting.
- Drawable retry 100 ms to 16 ms when a monitor connects before its first drawable. Ours is still 100 ms at `pvg_metal.m:2783`. Relevant to plugging in the external display.
- The texture mapper queue at user-interactive QoS.
- 1 MiB NAT socket buffers and net dequeue batched to 32 frames per lock were in the shortlist without a measurement.

Also: skarakose 533fcf1c and Tomba247 088aa322 build `tl-samples.dmg` with `SDKROOT`/`IPHONEOS_DEPLOYMENT_TARGET` unset. Our `henge-agent` target already does that; our `tl-samples.dmg` target (`Makefile:201`) does not. This is a build fix only, and only if the iOS build actually reaches that target.

### 8. Skipped after checking
- pgabove USB touchscreen on xHCI port 3. Our main already has 3 ports, and port 3 is the USB mouse (`devices/xhci.h:10-18`, a0cc62f), so this collides with our mouse.
- lunarduststudiodev-rgb 02913432 same-block backward branches as direct jumps. No test or measurement, and it flips `HV_SUPERBLOCKS` on with a broken off switch.
- lunarduststudiodev-rgb scroll constants (`topSpeed` 24000 to 8000, `scrollScale` 3 to 1.2). These are taste tuning and conflict with our own input work.
- lunarduststudiodev-rgb e855504e also deletes the thermal present throttle; don't take that.
- The virtio RX double-poll, the conn hash without tombstones, and the glide watcher change dropping the `glide &&` check are correctness risks.
- mohamedt1996 Android applegfx/Vulkan, muns5965 Android research, lingbing09 Apple Pencil (iPad), Lintel Windows board, Kali ISO, Codemagic CI, Tomba247 2 GB code cache (withdrawn by its own author).
